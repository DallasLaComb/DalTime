Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Overview

DalTime is a shift scheduling platform for organizations of all types, with a commitment to serving nonprofits. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the commitments we make to protect it.

What We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the scheduling platform:

  • Account details (name, email address, role)
  • Availability submissions and shift data entered through the application
  • Usage data generated while interacting with the platform

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party, ever. Your data exists to make the scheduling platform work for you and your organization, nothing else.

Analytics

Any analytics performed on application data are used solely to improve the performance, reliability, and efficiency of the platform. We do not use analytics to profile users, target advertising, or share insights with outside parties.

How Your Data Is Stored

DalTime is built on AWS. Your data is stored in Amazon DynamoDB, which encrypts all data at rest by default using AES-256 encryption managed by AWS. Data is also encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

We do not apply any additional encryption layer beyond what AWS provides out of the box. If you have specific compliance requirements, reach out before using the platform.

As the platform administrator, Dallas LaComb has the ability to view all data stored in the system. This access exists for operational and support purposes only.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your organization's account is active. If you would like your data removed, contact us at the email below and we will handle it promptly.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at contact@daltime.com.
