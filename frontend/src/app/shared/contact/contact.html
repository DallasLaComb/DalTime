Onboarding a New Organization

DalTime supports two tracks for new organizations:

Nonprofits: Email us to apply for free access. Include a brief description of your organization and team size. Approval is subject to the owner's review and is not guaranteed.

All other organizations: Reach out to learn about our free and paid tiers, or visit the Pricing page to explore your options. No approval required to get started.

Reporting a Problem

If you've found a bug or something isn't working as expected, please email us with as much detail as possible: what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. We take reliability seriously and will address issues as promptly as we can.

Response Time

DalTime is run by a single developer. We aim to respond to all inquiries within a few business days, though response times may vary. Thank you for your patience.
