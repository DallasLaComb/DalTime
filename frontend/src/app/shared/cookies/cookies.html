Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data stored in your browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember certain information about your session to improve how the application functions.

How DalTime Uses Cookies

DalTime uses cookies strictly for functional purposes, including:

  • Keeping you logged in during your session
  • Remembering your authentication state across page navigations
  • Supporting the secure operation of the platform

Cookies are not used for advertising, user profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the application.

We Do Not Sell Cookie Data

Cookie data collected by DalTime is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. It exists solely to make the platform work correctly for you.

Third-Party Cookies

DalTime does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. No external services are given access to your cookie data.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to stay logged in and use certain features of the platform.
