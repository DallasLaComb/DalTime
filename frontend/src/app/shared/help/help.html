Getting Started

How do I get access to DalTime?

DalTime is invitation-only. Your organization's admin will create your account and send you login credentials. If you haven't received an invitation, reach out to your manager or org admin.

If your organization is not yet on DalTime, approved nonprofits receive free access by contacting us. All other organizations can sign up via our free or paid tiers on the Pricing page.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password via email. If you're still having trouble, contact your org admin or email us at contact@daltime.com.

What are the different roles in DalTime?

DalTime has four roles:

  • Employee: submits availability, views their schedule, and manages shift swaps.
  • Manager: defines shifts, reviews availability, and generates schedules.
  • Org Admin: manages managers and employees across the organization.
  • Web Admin: DalTime platform-level administration.

Employees

How do I submit my availability?

Log in and navigate to your dashboard. From there you can submit or update your availability for the upcoming scheduling period. Your manager will use this to build the schedule.

How do I swap a shift?

Once schedules are published, you can post a shift for pickup from your dashboard. A teammate can then claim it. Shift swaps are visible to your manager.

Managers

How does auto-scheduling work?

Once employees have submitted their availability, you can generate a schedule with one click. The system respects availability windows, the 40-hour weekly limit for part-time staff, and any shift constraints you've defined.

Can I edit a generated schedule before publishing?

Yes. Generated schedules are drafts until you publish them. You can make manual adjustments before employees are able to see it.
